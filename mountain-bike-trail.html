Recreational Trail Construction • Eucha, Oklahoma

Mountain Bike
Trail

An approximately ¾-mile mountain bike trail built through extremely steep terrain, using the natural shape of the property to create a challenging and beautiful ride.

The Project

Turning steep Oklahoma terrain into a challenging ¾-mile trail.

The owner gave Rafter E creative freedom to use as much of the property as needed to lay out and build the trail. That allowed the route to work with the natural terrain and make full use of the property.

The job was completed in two phases, with each phase taking approximately 2–3 days. The biggest challenge was the extremely steep terrain, which required careful equipment work and a trail layout that followed the natural contours of the property.

Building the Trail

Working with the terrain.

The Result

A challenging trail in a beautiful setting.

The freedom to use the property creatively made it possible to build a trail that takes advantage of the steep terrain and natural setting instead of fighting against it.

“What I’m most proud of is how amazing the trail turned out. The owner gave me creative freedom to use as much of the property as needed, which allowed me to build a trail that was both challenging and beautiful.”

— Jason Evans, Rafter E LLC
