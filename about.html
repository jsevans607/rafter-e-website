About Rafter E

Built on experience.
Grounded in integrity.

Rafter E is an owner-managed, veteran-owned company focused on helping property owners prepare land for what comes next.

Our Story

Building the Foundation for What’s Next.

From Raw Land to Ready Ground through Experience, Integrity, and the Right Equipment.

Rafter E grew from years of construction and heavy-equipment experience. After working in the industry and seeing the needs around Northeast Oklahoma, Jason and Sheryl Evans built a company around practical solutions, honest communication and visible results.

The work is rewarding because every project has a clear outcome: an overgrown property becomes usable, a rough site becomes ready for construction, or a damaged shoreline becomes accessible again.

Our goal is not simply to move dirt or remove trees. It is to understand what the owner wants the property to become and prepare it correctly for that next step.
